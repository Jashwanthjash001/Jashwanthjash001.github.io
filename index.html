About

I'm an AI engineer who likes taking large language models out of the cloud and running them on hardware people already own. Most of my recent work is fully on-device — quantizing models to fit a laptop, serving them, building real apps on them, and wiring agents on top.

I care about this because it's where a lot of teams actually need LLMs: when compliance, cost, or latency rule out sending data to a hosted model. I like projects where I can measure the thing instead of guessing — a perplexity-vs-speed table, a latency curve, a before/after exact-match number — and keep honest notes about the limitations.

Right now I'm going deeper on inference optimization (batching internals, KV-cache, CUDA) and fine-tuning beyond toy tasks.
